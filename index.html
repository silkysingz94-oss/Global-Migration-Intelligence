Methodology and data limitations

  • Net migration is immigration minus emigration.
  • Positive balances indicate net migration gains.
  • Negative balances indicate net migration losses.
  • UN WPP 2024 historical estimates cover 1950–2023.
  • Uploaded projections must be distinguished from historical estimates.
  • National figures cover migration from all origins and to all destinations.
  • These estimates do not measure Gambian-origin migration specifically.
  • Missing observations are not interpreted as zero.
